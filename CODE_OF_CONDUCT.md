# Community Code of Conduct

We want HTML6 to be a welcoming learning and development community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Welcome beginners and questions.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Share feedback in a way that helps others learn.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Contact the project maintainers privately through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers should review reports fairly and take proportionate action.
